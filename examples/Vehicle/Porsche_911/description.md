# Evolution of the Porsche 911

## Scope
Every 911 generation, from the 1963 original to the 992.2.

## Lineage
Base Carrera (or equivalent base 911) of each generation and mid-cycle update.

## Metrics
- `power`: Rated power, in PS.
- `weight`: Curb weight (DIN), in kg.
- `price`: US base MSRP at launch, nominal USD.
- `acceleration_0_100`: 0-100 km/h, in seconds.
- `top_speed`: Top speed, in km/h.
- `drag`: Drag coefficient (Cd).
- `fuel_consumption`: Combined fuel consumption, in L/100 km.

Rows marked `speculation=true` are forecasts, not data.

## Known gaps

## Release cycle
- **Cadence:** 84–96 months
- **Usual timing:** Autumn reveal (LA or Frankfurt shows historically).
- **Where to check:** [Porsche Newsroom](https://newsroom.porsche.com/en.html)
- 991 (2011), 992 (2019); mid-cycle facelifts (992.2 in 2024) about 4 years after launch.
